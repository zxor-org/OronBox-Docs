## Home-page placements

The OronBox Home tab has several sections:

- **Daily featured**: editor-picked resources, marked **Featured**.
- **Daily recommended**: the recommendation slot.
- **Recent updates**: resources published or updated recently.
- **News & editor sections**: announcements, recommendations and editor-maintained sections (e.g. banner carousel).

## How resources get exposure

- **Featured / recommended slots** are picked by the OronBox editors; quality and compliance come first.
- **Recent updates** scrolls naturally by publish time — keep updating and your resource stays visible.
- **Coins increase exposure**: the more coins a resource receives, the more likely it shows up in recommendations.

## What you can do

- Keep quality and compliance high (see [Review & status](./review)) to be picked by the editors.
- Release updates regularly so the resource stays in "Recent updates".
- Use **collections** to present a series (see [Collections](./collections)).
- Encourage users to give coins — they help both you and the resource's exposure.

## Note

The exact ranking rules are decided platform-side.
