## Where coins come from

Users mainly earn coins through the **daily check-in**: 1–5 coins per day. The balance and check-in entry live on the Home tab → "Resource coins".

## How users give coins

On a resource detail page, users tap **Coin** to give 1 or 2 coins:

- Coins can't be taken back.
- Coins help the resource get **more exposure** (see [Recommendation](./recommend)).
- Creators receive **10%** of the coins given as earnings.

## Creator earnings

The creator center → "Creation rewards" shows:

- **Lifetime coins received**: the total across all your resources.
- **Coins in the last 14 days**: recent activity.

Earnings come from coins given to any of your resources, regardless of platform. Coins are an in-platform reward points system, not cash.

## Notes

- Coins are a signal of appreciation; quality and consistent updates drive long-term support.
- Collection cards show total coins received, another entry point to your work.
