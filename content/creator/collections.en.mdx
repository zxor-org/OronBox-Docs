title: Collections
description: Group similar resources into a collection card for more exposure
---

Collections group several of your resources into a single card, making them easier to browse and easier to discover.

## Creating a collection

In the creator center → "Collections" → "New collection":

1. Fill in the **collection name** and **summary**.
2. Pick a **representative resource** (the card's cover).
3. Move resources **into the collection**, or add them while editing.
4. Reorder the resources by **dragging**.
5. Submit; the collection info goes through review and becomes public once approved.

## Managing a collection

- **Edit collection info**: name, summary, representative resource, etc. (changes are re-reviewed).
- **Manage resources**: add or remove resources.
- **Dissolve**: remove the association from all resources.
- **Delete**: the collection is deleted; resources are kept, only the association is removed.

## What users see

- Resources inside a collection are tagged "From collection \{name\}" on their detail page.
- The collection card shows the resource count and total coins received, a good entry point to your series.
