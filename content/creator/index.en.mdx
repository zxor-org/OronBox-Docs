The creator center is where resource authors publish watchfaces, quick apps and more, manage their status, group resources into collections, and earn coins.

## Getting in

The creator center needs a **BandBBS account with the OronBox account linked**. The first time you enter:

1. Read and accept the **BandBBS community terms** and the **AstroBox-Repo submission guidelines**.
2. Authorize OronBox to publish BandBBS resources on your behalf (BandBBS write permission).
3. Optionally connect your GitHub account to publish AstroBox resources under your own name.

Without a sign-in, the entry says "Sign in to use the creator center".

## The workspace

The creator center contains:

- **My resources**: Drafts / Pending review / Published
- **Collections**: group your resources into collection cards
- **Creation rewards**: lifetime coins received, coins received in the last 14 days
- **Import**: import your existing resources from BandBBS / AstroBox (experimental)

- [Review & status](/en/creator/review): understand the review flow and each status
- [Collections](/en/creator/collections): group similar resources into a collection
- [Coins & rewards](/en/creator/coins): check-in, coins and creator earnings
- [Recommendation](/en/creator/recommend): how resources get more exposure
- [Limits](/en/creator/limits): name, image, file and device requirements

## Banned / frozen accounts

- **Account banned**: the creator center is unavailable; contact the admins via a support ticket.
- **Creator features frozen**: you can't submit or manage resources temporarily; the rest of the account works normally.
