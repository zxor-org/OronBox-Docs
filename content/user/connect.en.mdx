## Supported devices

OronBox identifies devices by their Bluetooth name:

**Xiaomi / Redmi (VelaOS)**: Mi Band 8 Pro, 9, 9 Pro, 10, 10 Pro; Xiaomi Watch S1 Pro, S3, S4, S5; Redmi Watch 4, 5, 5 eSIM, 6.

**ZeppOS**: Amazfit Active, Balance, Band 7, Bip 5/6, Cheetah, GTR 3/4, GTS 3/4, T-Rex 2/3/Ultra, Helio Ring and more. Note that **Mi Band 7 (Xiaomi Smart Band 7) is a ZeppOS device** and connects the ZeppOS way.

If the Bluetooth name doesn't match any known device, it shows "Unrecognized device" — you can still try to connect manually.

## Connection methods

- **Xiaomi / Redmi**: classic Bluetooth (SPP). Turn on "Connect to a new phone" on the device first (usually in the band/watch Bluetooth settings).
- **ZeppOS**: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). No system Bluetooth pairing needed.
- **Web**: Xiaomi-family devices connect over Web Serial (browser serial API); ZeppOS devices over Web Bluetooth.

## First connection (step by step)

1. Open the **Devices** tab and tap **Scan and add**.
2. Wait for the scan results (it auto-stops after 15 seconds; scan again if nothing shows up).
3. Tap your device in the **Discovered devices** list.
4. Enter the device's **authkey** and connect.
5. The connection progresses through: preparing → establishing connection → initializing the device protocol → authenticating the device → reading device info.
6. Once connected, the device is saved to **Paired devices**, and you can connect to it with a single tap afterwards.

> If the device was connected by the official client or another tool before, disconnect it there first — a Bluetooth device is usually only reachable by one connection at a time.

## The authkey

The authkey authenticates the device; both device families need it when connecting.

- **Xiaomi devices**: after signing into your Xiaomi account, bound devices' authkeys can be synced automatically (Settings → Accounts → Xiaomi → "Sign in and sync"). You can also type it manually.
- **ZeppOS devices**: the authkey is a 32-character hexadecimal string; leave it empty if the device still uses the default key.

The authkey is stored locally (in the current browser on the web), unencrypted. Don't enter it on shared machines.

## Web Serial (connecting Xiaomi devices from the browser)

1. On the Devices page, open "Connect via Web Serial".
2. Enter the authkey and pick the device's serial port in the browser dialog.
3. Saved devices and authkeys stay in the current browser; switching browsers or clearing site data means reconnecting.

## Auto-reconnect

Settings → Connection & downloads → Auto-reconnect reconnects the last paired device on startup (off by default; not supported on the web).

## When connecting fails

- "Bluetooth is unavailable. Please check that Bluetooth is enabled and the system permission allows OronBox to use it." — Bluetooth is off or not permitted. On macOS, allow the permission prompt on first launch.
- "Connection failed. Please make sure Bluetooth permission is granted and Bluetooth is on, the device is nearby and not in use by another tool or device, and the device-side 'Connect to a new phone' mode is enabled." — the device wasn't found or is busy; check each item above.
- "Bluetooth connection lost, please reconnect the device." — the connection dropped; reconnect.
- "No usable transport endpoint found. Complete system Bluetooth pairing first, then rescan." — pair the device in the system Bluetooth settings first, then rescan.
