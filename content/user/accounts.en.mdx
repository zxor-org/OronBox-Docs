title: Accounts
description: BandBBS, Xiaomi, Huami and GitHub accounts — what each is for and how to sign in
---

Accounts live under Settings → Accounts & authorization. There are four, each with a different purpose:

| Account | Sign-in method | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BandBBS | Browser authorization | BandBBS resources, comments, creator center |
| Xiaomi | Username + password | Sync bound Xiaomi devices (with authkeys) |
| Huami | Username + password | Huami app store resources |
| GitHub | Browser authorization (BandBBS first) | Publish AstroBox resources under your own account |

Step 3 of the first-run wizard also offers sign-in; you can skip it and sign in later from Settings.

## BandBBS

Tap "Sign in with BandBBS" and complete the authorization in your browser. After signing in you can:

- Access BandBBS community resources and take part in comments.
- Look up **purchased resources** and install paid ones you own (BandBBS account page → Purchased resources → enter the resource ID).
- Use the [Creator center](/en/creator) to publish resources.

Re-sign in when the session expires.

## Xiaomi

Enter your username and password; sign-in happens locally in the app.

- "Sign in and sync" imports the Xiaomi devices bound to your account — **only devices that carry an authkey are imported**, and you can then connect to them directly.
- If **two-factor authentication (2FA)** is enabled, the app opens the verification page in an embedded window; sign-in continues after verification.
- **2FA is not supported on the web**; an account with 2FA cannot sign in from the browser.
- Credentials are saved only if you tick "Remember username and password".

## Huami

Enter your username and password to store the credentials the Zepp store needs, used for Huami app store resources.

## GitHub

Used to publish AstroBox resources under your own GitHub account:

- **Sign into BandBBS first**: without it, the GitHub entry shows "Connect after signing into BandBBS", because the GitHub sign-in depends on the BandBBS session.
- Tap connect and complete the GitHub authorization in your browser; the app continues automatically.

## Common errors

- "Xiaomi account or password is incorrect." — wrong credentials.
- "Xiaomi two-factor verification incomplete. Please sign in again." — the 2FA flow was interrupted; sign in again and complete verification.
- "OronBox sign-in expired, please sign in to BandBBS again." — the BandBBS session expired; sign in again.
